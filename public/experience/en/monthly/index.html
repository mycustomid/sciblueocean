LONG STAYS

A place to live, a story to build.

Because some plans take time and every new chapter of life deserves a setting to match, we imagine spaces where you can feel at home, thrive and build your everyday life.

We offer one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments designed for individuals, couples and families alike. Comfortable, functional and carefully furnished spaces support your plans, welcome your family, help you pursue your ambitions and create precious memories.

Residences designed for your comfort

Our residences combine contemporary architecture, elegance, modern design and the latest technologies. Every detail is considered to create a refined, practical and secure living environment.

Our apartments benefit from carefully selected fittings and amenities:

  • Quality materials and premium finishes.
  • Optimised spaces and functional layouts.
  • Smart home installations to make everyday life easier.
  • Double-glazed windows for improved acoustic and thermal comfort.
  • Surveillance cameras and security systems.
  • Lifts to make moving around easier.
  • A generator to ensure a continuous electricity supply.
  • An independent water supply system using a borehole.

More than a residence, a true art of living

Because well-being does not end at your apartment door, our residences also include spaces for relaxation, leisure and spending time together:

  • A gym to support your fitness and well-being.
  • A children's play area, designed for their development and time together.
  • A padel court for sports and outdoor activity enthusiasts.
  • A furnished rooftop, ideal for relaxing, meeting and sharing special moments.

Our residences

Résidence Miague, Résidence Ebène and Résidence Amara embody our vision of modern, comfortable and sustainable living.

These residences are at different stages of development. Some are currently under construction and will gradually expand our residential offering.

Our ambition: to offer you more than a roof. A space to live, grow, pursue your ambitions, share and write the most beautiful chapters of your story.
