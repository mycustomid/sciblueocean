Clear conditions. No surprises.

This information guides your request. The operator must approve final terms, rates and property policies.

01

Reservations

A request does not hold a room. Staff confirm each reservation after checking availability.

02

Pricing and payment

Shown rates are indicative starting prices in FCFA. The final amount must be stated in the booking confirmation.

03

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation and refund terms are disclosed before payment. Unverified policy language is not contractual.

04

Privacy

Contact details are recorded to process your request. Contact the property for access, correction or deletion requests. Data controller and retention information still needs to be completed.
