07 / PARTIAL RECEIPTS & SHIPMENTS · RC9

Deliver in stages. Account for every movement.

Select an approved purchase/sales order, inspect its open quantities, and receive or ship one item at a time. Every confirmed event produces an immutable stock movement and an independent draft journal; retries use an idempotency key.
