Read all booking policies
Which residence can I book today?+

The private studio at Résidence Miague is open for reservation requests. Its larger apartments are currently occupied. Availability for your dates is checked before you send a request.

Can I rent an apartment by the month?+

Yes. You can register interest in monthly apartments at Résidence Ebène and Résidence Amara. Both are under construction, so preferred move in dates are subject to delivery and final confirmation.

How does booking work?+

Choose your dates, review the estimate and send your reservation request. Track confirmation and speak with the property team from My bookings. No online payment is collected with the request.

What are the arrival and departure times?+

Check in begins at 2 PM and check out is at 12 PM, local Cameroon time. Late departure may be arranged with reception, subject to availability and a fee.
